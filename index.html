Background

History so far!!!

I completed my PhD at the University of Western Australia, where I built context-inspired models across healthcare and sport before turning to mRNA technology. The thread through all of it is efficiency: systems designed to work with the resources a real clinic or team actually has, not an idealised one.

Outside of research code, I spend a lot of time outdoors — hiking, wildlife, and photography keep the "does this actually work in the real world" instinct sharp.

Dream: to see the whole world and experience every culture.
